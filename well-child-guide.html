Well-Child Visit Guide

Newborn through 21 years

Each visit page lists what to ask, what to screen, which vaccines are due, where to focus the exam, and what to discuss with the family. Screening follows the Bright Futures/AAP periodicity schedule, milestones follow the CDC 2022 checklists, and vaccines follow the AAP 2026 schedule.

For a focused visit, see the Pediatric Focused History.

Physical exam by age

The sequence follows standard structured clinical observation forms. The approach changes more with age than the content does.

Approach

  1. Gather as much as possible by observation before touching the child: color, work of breathing, tone, alertness, interaction with the parent.
  2. Examine infants and toddlers on the parent's lap when that keeps them calm. Stay at the child's eye level.
  3. Go from least to most distressing. Auscultate heart and lungs while the child is quiet; leave ears, mouth, and hips for last.
  4. Examine a painful area last, after you have a sense of the child's overall state.
  5. Be honest. If something will hurt, say so calmly. Use distraction freely with toddlers.
  6. Expect stranger anxiety from about 9 to 18 months. A child who cooperated at 6 months may not at 12.
  7. Offer a chaperone for sensitive exams and keep areas covered when you are not examining them.

Vital signs by age

Count respirations for a full 60 seconds in infants; periodic breathing makes shorter counts unreliable. Count pulse for at least 30 seconds. Use a cuff whose bladder width is about 40% of the arm circumference.

Immunization note, October 2026. In January 2026 the CDC moved several routine vaccines (hepatitis A and B, rotavirus, influenza, COVID-19, meningococcal, RSV antibody) to shared clinical decision-making. A federal court stayed that change in March 2026 and restored the prior CDC schedule; the government's appeal is pending in the First Circuit. This guide follows the AAP 2026 schedule, which is endorsed by AAFP, ACOG, AMA, IDSA, and others. New York State also requires blood lead testing at 1 and 2 years. Check the current schedules before clinic.
