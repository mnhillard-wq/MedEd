Post-op appendectomy, ready for discharge

Two steps. A 19-year-old college student on post-operative day 2 after laparoscopic appendectomy for uncomplicated appendicitis. The learner pre-rounds at the bedside, confirming bowel function, diet, pain, walking, and the wound with a patient who volunteers worries but not symptoms, then presents to Dr. Park and makes the discharge decision. Elena Vasquez

Post-op appendectomy with ileus, not ready for discharge

Two steps. A 54-year-old teacher on post-operative day 3 after laparoscopic appendectomy for phlegmonous appendicitis who says she is fine. The learner pre-rounds and draws out what she minimizes: no gas or stool, nausea with abdominal pressure, a failed meal, dizziness when walking. The learner then presents to Dr. Park, recognizes post-operative ileus, holds the diet, and keeps her in. Pairs with Marcus Webb.

Primary skillPre-rounding & Oral Presentation
Learner activityPre-round + Attending Debrief
AttendingDr. Park

Cannot missAdvancing the diet or discharging before bowel function returns; missing vomiting, rebound, or rising fever as signs of more than ileus.
