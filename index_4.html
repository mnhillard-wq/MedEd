Cases by clerkship

Each clerkship page lists its practice cases. On every case you can try the case if it is ready, open the proposed rubric, and send comments or suggested changes.

How to review a case
  1. Open a clerkship and pick a case.
  2. Try it as a learner would, if it is marked ready.
  3. Open the proposed rubric. It opens in Notion and needs faculty access.
  4. Type your comments on the card and send them to the feedback form.
