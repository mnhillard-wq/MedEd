Presentation coach · Pediatrics · MS3-4

Dr. Alvarez: Inpatient pediatrics, new admission

Bring a patient you have seen. Dr. Alvarez listens to your whole oral presentation without interrupting, then coaches its structure: what to lead with, what was missing, and what to tighten. The coach does not judge the clinical content; review that with your attending.

Faculty preview. This coach is in development and may not yet behave as intended.

What Dr. Alvarez listens for
  • D1 Opening Statement and Organization
  • D2 HPI
  • D3 Past and Specialty History (PMH, PSH, birth and perinatal, developmental, feeding, immunizations, growth)
  • D4 Medications, Allergies, Social and Family History
  • D5 Review of Systems
  • D6 Physical Exam
  • D7 Diagnostic Data
  • D8 Summary Statement
  • D9 Assessment Framing
  • D10 Plan Framing
  • D11 Delivery and Communication
